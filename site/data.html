    How to read this

    What these numbers can and cannot tell

    Best quality only

    We keep a reading only if SWOT's quality flag is 0, its crossover flag is 0 or 1, and at most 10% of the lake is "dark water" (Köhn and Nielsen, 2026).

    Change, not the whole tank

    SWOT's storage numbers show how much water came and went. How full a tank is also needs the water below SWOT's lowest reading, from the Irrigation Department's capacity tables.

    Not yet checked

    Nobody has checked SWOT's storage numbers against Sri Lankan gauges. Doing that, and building our own curves, is the core of our project.

    Data made on from NASA PO.DAAC's Hydrocron (SWOT Lake Single-Pass product, version D). Official capacities: Irrigation Department, Reservoir Status, 25 Oct 2026. You can also see SWOT readings for any lake on the public SWOT Lake and River Explorer.

Team

Who does what

Three members, each with a research question and one model to train: a water map, a SWOT trust model and a storage forecast. For each one: the data analysis and the technical work.

Member A

AI water maps

RQ1

How accurately can deep learning map tank water from Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2, even under cloud or water plants?

Data analysis

  • Label water masks of our tanks, with a "water under plants" class.
  • Score the maps: IoU and F1 against the labels.
  • Area error against Kanhindawewa's drone survey, by sensor, season and plant cover.
  • Compare with the simple rules and with SWOT's own area on the same days.

Technical work

  • Model: a U-Net (EfficientNet-B0) trained on the S1S2-Water dataset, then fine-tuned on our tanks.
  • Inputs: Sentinel-1 VV and VH, NISAR L-band radar, Sentinel-2, slope.
  • Pipeline: find each picture day (Picture days), download it, map the water, measure the area.

Output: water area for every tank on every picture day, with its accuracy.

Member B

SWOT and gauges

RQ2

How accurate is SWOT on reservoirs and village tanks, and can a learned model tell which readings to trust better than SWOT's own flags?

Data analysis

  • SWOT minus gauge on every pass: the average gap and the spread in cm.
  • Labels for the model: each reading's error at the 117 department reservoirs.
  • The trust model against SWOT's own flags, on reservoirs it never saw.
  • How a height error turns into a volume error.

Technical work

  • Hydrocron pipeline: each tank's lake ID, every pass (SWOT data).
  • Model: a SWOT trust model, gradient boosting (XGBoost or LightGBM) on SWOT's quality fields, track distance, lake size and season.
  • Gauge zero in metres above sea level: full supply level minus full supply depth.
  • Daily logger for the department's sheet; storage history turned back into gauge heights.

Output: a SWOT accuracy report, the fixed gap for each reservoir, and a trust model that scores every reading.

Member C

Storage and forecast

RQ3RQ4

Does AI water area plus SWOT height give storage close to official values, and can a deep-learning model forecast it 2–4 weeks ahead?

Data analysis

  • Each tank's area–height curve A(h) from its dots.
  • Storage change against the department's daily storage.
  • Test the method on Kanhindawewa's drone table, and against Imbulana's formula.
  • Forecast error at 1, 2 and 4 weeks, against "no change" and the seasonal average.

Technical work

  • Storage engine: dots, curve, thin layers and safe limits, after every pass (Step by step).
  • Model: a storage forecast, an LSTM with a gradient-boosting baseline, trained on 117 reservoirs' daily storage (2020–2026), rain and evaporation.
  • Dashboard: storage, safe limits and the forecast for each tank.
  • Automatic updates with GitHub Actions.

Output: storage for every tank with safe limits, and a 2–4 week forecast, on a dashboard.

All three: field visits on SWOT pass dates, labelling water masks, the thesis and the research paper.

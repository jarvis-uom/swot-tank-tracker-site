Past research

How others measured water from space

What earlier studies used, how accurate they were, what goes wrong for our small tanks, and our answer. Every number here was checked against the study itself in October 2026.

In short

What the studies tell us

Heights are good

SWOT reads water height to within about 5 to 20 cm. The big reservoirs also have a gauge reading every day.

Area and shape cause the error

In India, a 17% area error became a 31% volume error. In Burkina Faso, water plants spoiled 6 of 20 reservoirs.

Nobody has done our tanks

We found no study that tracks the water in Sri Lankan tanks with SWOT. We searched in October 2026.

1. Water height
Madumalsha et al., 20259 Sri Lankan reservoirsChecked: full paper ICESat-2 laser heights, compared with the gauges Correlation 0.99, but 37 cm too high on average It passes every 91 days: only 3 to 8 readings per reservoir in 5 years SWOT about twice a month, and the daily gauge at the 5 big reservoirs. We remove each reservoir's fixed gap of 40 to 64 cm.
Köhn and Nielsen, 202637 lakes in Denmark, 25 ha to 40 km²Checked: full paper SWOT heights, cleaned with SWOT's quality flags 5 to 6 cm Heights only, no volume. Their smallest lake is bigger than our village tanks. We clean SWOT with a rule based on theirs, then add the water area to get the volume.
2. Water area
SWOT team, 2025 and Girard et al., 2025Lakes worldwide; 16 lakes in West AfricaChecked: slides; abstract SWOT's own water area Off by 19 to 26% on small lakes, mostly too small (SWOT team). 17% too big in West Africa (Girard). At Mahakanadarawa, SWOT saw 705 ha when the photo showed 1,113 ha (10 March 2024) SWOT gives only the height. The area comes from radar and photos. Girard did this with Sentinel-2 photos: curves within 28 cm.
Perera and Jayaweera, 2020 and Rajakaruna et al., 20236 Sri Lankan reservoirsChecked: abstract; full paper Sentinel-1 radar water area Area against reported volume: R² 0.67 to 0.94 (Perera). Not checked against the ground (Rajakaruna). Area only: no height and no curve. Sentinel-2 photos were mostly lost to cloud. Give each picture day a height, so it becomes a dot on the tank's curve. Check against the department's daily storage.
IFAD and ESA, 2026Over 10,650 water bodies in 6 Sri Lankan districtsChecked: web page Monthly 10 m water maps, 2017 to August 2025 Not stated on the page Water extent only: no height and no volume Turn the area into volume with heights and the tank's curve A(h).
Wieland et al., 2024S1S2-Water: 65 areas in 29 countriesChecked: full paper AI water maps (U-Net) from Sentinel-1 radar and Sentinel-2 photos Overlap with the true water: 97% (photos), 85% (radar) Radar mixes up sand and bare soil with water. There is no "water under plants" class. Train on our tanks with a "water under plants" class. Test on Kanhindawewa, where a drone survey gives the true area at every height.
Dossou Codjia et al., 202520 reservoirs in Burkina FasoChecked: abstract Sentinel-2 photos and a Random Forest for the area, then area against the gauge level R² 0.76 to 0.97 at 14 of the 20 At the other 6, water plants hid the water, so the area came out too small. The "water under plants" class, and plant-covered tanks reported on their own.
3. The tank's shape: from area to volume
Pascal et al., 2024504 small reservoirs in Telangana, IndiaChecked: full paper Sentinel-2 area, and bed maps made from satellite photos while the tanks were empty A 17% area error became a 31% volume error. Global elevation maps: 47 to 78% off. Area errors grow into bigger volume errors. Photos miss water at the edges and under plants. Better area from radar and AI, and safe limits on every volume (see Step by step).
Imbulana, 2022About 150 Sri Lankan village tanksChecked: full paper A simple formula: the full area, depth and capacity give the volume at any level Under 5% off at depths above one-third On Kanhindawewa's drone survey it gave 4 to 14% too much water, and was up to 24% off near the bottom (our test). A backup only. Each tank's curve comes from its own dots.
GRDL, 2024 and 3D-LAKES, 2025Thousands of lakes and reservoirs worldwideChecked: abstracts Lake beds guessed by AI from elevation data (GRDL), or from ICESat-2 and Landsat (3D-LAKES) Depth off by about 8 m (GRDL). Curves off by 60 cm (3D-LAKES). Too rough: our tanks rise and fall only about 1 to 5 m a year. Each tank's own curve from its own dots, tied to the gauge.
4. Storage with SWOT
Das and Hossain, 2025245 reservoirs in 5 countriesChecked: full paper SWOT heights with Sentinel-1, Sentinel-2 and Landsat areas; curves from SRTM elevation data SWOT height R² 0.91 (usual miss 92 cm). Storage R² above 0.8. Mostly big reservoirs. SRTM curves can be off by a few metres. Area-based storage did badly in monsoon Thailand. Our curve comes from our own dots, not SRTM. Radar works in the monsoon.
Li et al., 202614 reservoirs, mostly in the USA and MexicoChecked: full paper SWOT heights with global curves (GRDL, REGEOM) Storage error 15% of capacity; 3% once tied to each reservoir's own records The tie needs local records, which most tanks don't have. We tie SWOT to each big reservoir's gauge: gauge zero = full supply level minus full depth. Village tanks are tested on Kanhindawewa.
Ma et al., 202612 reservoirs in the western USAChecked: abstract SWOT height and area from the same pass, and a reservoir model for the gaps Height within 20 cm; storage within 10% (median) Big US reservoirs. The gaps are filled by a model, not by data. We fill the gaps with real pictures: radar every few days. The department's daily storage is our check.
5. Between SWOT passes
de Fleury et al., 2023 and Zhan et al., 202637 small lakes in the Sahel; 8 reservoirs in ChinaChecked: full paper; abstract On days with only a picture, the height is read off the tank's area–height curve Curves within 2 to 7 cm on 2 lakes (de Fleury). Error 11 to 46 cm, with about 121 levels a year (Zhan). de Fleury used photos only, and clouds hide most photos here. Zhan's reservoirs are large. Every radar day or clear photo becomes a dot, with a height from the gauge or the line between two SWOT passes: layers about 4 cm thick in 2026 (see Picture days).
Wu et al., 20237 small reservoirs, 4 of them in CaliforniaChecked: full paper AI (ResNet34) that reads the water level straight from Sentinel-1 and Sentinel-2 images Error 19 to 77 cm; R² 0.62 to 0.97 It learns from each reservoir's own gauge readings, which village tanks don't have. No SWOT. SWOT gives heights where there is no gauge, so every tank gets dots.
